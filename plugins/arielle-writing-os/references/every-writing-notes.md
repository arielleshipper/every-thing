## Katie Parrott Patterns

- Opens from a concrete personal or work experiment.
- Uses a precise, sometimes funny detail to make the abstract real.
- Treats AI as a force changing work, identity, ergonomics, and judgment.
- Lets the piece widen from "what happened to me" to "what this reveals about work."
- Often names the awkwardness or fear directly instead of smoothing it.

## Dan Shipper Patterns

- States the thesis plainly.
- Explains the operating logic of the company, product, or market.
- Uses concrete numbers and business context when available.
- Makes the reader understand why the moment matters.
- Treats Every as an unusual system rather than a conventional media or software company.
