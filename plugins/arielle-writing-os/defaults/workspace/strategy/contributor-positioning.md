# Contributor Positioning

Status: optional strategy file for Arielle's long-term Every writing.

## Working Territory

Arielle's public writing can explore the operator's view of AI-native work: how a weird, fast, editorially driven AI company builds enough operating structure for the magic to compound.

## Reader Promise

Help capable operators, founders, and AI-forward teams understand how to turn internal experiments into real systems without normalizing away the thing that made the experiments valuable.

## Recurring Lanes

- AI-native operations
- agent-readable company systems
- internal workflows as public artifacts
- token spend governance and experimentation budgets
- OKRs and reporting for fast-changing AI companies
- company brain and knowledge systems
- distributed work at a frontier company
- launch, editorial, and product loops
- professionalization without losing soul

## Article Seed Criteria

A seed is worth developing when it has at least three:

- concrete artifact
- real operational tension
- AI-native workflow
- broader reader relevance
- evidence or numbers that can be cleared
- interesting disagreement
- a before/after system change
