# Voice

Status: user-owned cross-format voice guide.

This file captures stable preferences that should travel across destinations.

## Core Sound

My prose should feel:

- warm but not gushy
- clear but not cold
- competent without sounding like a corporate template
- practical, specific, and momentum-oriented
- human enough to preserve judgment and taste

My prose should not feel:

- generic
- over-smoothed
- consultant-y
- performatively polished
- evasive about the actual ask or claim

## Sentence Rhythm

Prefer short paragraphs and direct movement.

Use longer sentences when they hold a real nuance, tension, or conditional judgment.

Use short release lines sparingly for emphasis.

## Diction

Prefer plain English, operational specificity, and concrete nouns.

Avoid:

- circling back
- aligning stakeholders
- best-in-class
- please advise
- gentle reminder
- friendly nudge
- touching base
- at your earliest convenience

## Humor

Use light humor only when it lowers friction or preserves humanity. Prefer understatement, self-awareness, and precise absurdity over jokes.

## Guardrails

Preserve:

- a clear recommendation
- the real blocker or decision
- the human texture of the situation
- productive uncertainty when the answer is not settled

Remove:

- filler intros
- fake certainty
- generic AI transitions
- vague endings with no next step
