# Ops Style Guide

Status: destination guide for operational communication.

Use for Slack, email, Notion docs, policies, decision memos, handoffs, vendor messages, finance/compliance notes, and internal updates.

## Job

Operational writing should reduce coordination load.

It should make clear:

- what changed
- why it matters
- who owns what
- what decision or action is needed
- what risk or approval gate exists

## Tone

Use warmth, specificity, and polite firmness.

Sound like a capable operator, not a template.

## Common Structures

### Slack

Lead with the status or ask. Use bullets for details. End with owner/action/timing.

### Email

Use a human opener only if it earns its place. Make the ask clear. Keep next steps concrete.

### Memo / Policy

Lead with recommendation or rule. Explain reason and edge cases. Separate decision from background.

### Leadership Update

Use:

- headline
- what changed
- decision needed
- risk
- next move

## Avoid

- burying the ask
- over-explaining shared context
- passive-aggressive escalation
- vague "alignment" language
- process words that do not clarify ownership
