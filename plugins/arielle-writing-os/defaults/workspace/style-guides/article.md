# Every Article Style Guide

Status: destination guide for Arielle's public Every writing.

Use for Every articles, contributor drafts, Vibe Checks, Working Overtime-style pieces, Source Code-style experiments, and article pitches.

## Job

Turn operational experience into a useful public insight.

Every readers should leave with:

- a sharper frame
- a concrete example or artifact
- a workflow, question, or mental model they can use
- a sense of why this moment in AI/work matters

## Arielle's Likely Advantage

Arielle sees how the company actually works: systems, handoffs, spend, onboarding, goals, launch friction, product/editorial loops, and the gap between magical autonomy and necessary operating structure.

Use that operator vantage point as the source of originality.

## Default Shape

1. Concrete scene, artifact, or experiment.
2. The tension it exposed.
3. Why normal operating advice does not quite fit Every / AI-native work.
4. What Arielle tried or observed.
5. What changed.
6. What a capable reader can use.

## Evidence

Prefer:

- screenshots or artifacts when shareable
- before/after workflows
- numbers when approved
- Slack/Notion/process examples summarized safely
- decisions and tradeoffs

Do not expose private internal material without explicit approval.

## Style

Borrow structural lessons from Katie Parrott and Dan Shipper without imitating either:

- Katie: concrete experiment, human threshold, work/AI identity tension, useful weird detail.
- Dan: explicit thesis, systems-level logic, direct claims, company strategy, numbers where available.

The final piece should sound like Arielle: operational, clear, warm, specific, and willing to name a tension plainly.

## Avoid

- generic AI thought leadership
- ungrounded "future of work" claims
- making Every sound normal
- polishing away the strange or useful part
- publishing internal details before they are cleared

## Useful Formats

- Vibe Check observation
- operator case study
- Every internal experiment
- strategy memo turned essay
- debate/threshold piece
- workflow guide
